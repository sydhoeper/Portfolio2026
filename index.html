About

I find the structure inside messy problems.

I’m a senior product designer who likes products with real complexity: dense information, high-stakes decisions, multiple platforms, and users who cannot afford confusion.

My practice combines research, systems thinking, interaction design, prototyping, and close partnership with product and engineering. I’ve worked as a sole designer, embedded on cross-functional teams, and as a principal experience lead.

I’m currently completing an MFA in Game Design and Interactive Media at the University of Montana, exploring new forms of interaction through Unity, mixed reality, and AI-assisted workflows.
